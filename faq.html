WE ARE HERE TO HELP

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is NEXTAG?

NEXTAG is a sample catalog website for purses and wallets.

How can I find products?

Visit the Products page and use the search box or filters.

Can I filter products by brand?

Yes. Choose a brand from the dropdown on the Products page.

Can I compare products?

Yes. Add up to three products to your comparison list.

Can I buy products from this website?

No. This is a front-end educational project without checkout.

How can I contact the company?

Visit the Contact page to see the sample contact form.
